# Installing a screen at a business

**Bring:** the prepared tablet (see [tablet-setup.md](tablet-setup.md)), its wall charger, a 2 m+ cable (right-angle plug if it's in a frame), the mount or enclosure, cable clips, your phone signed in to the dashboard, the maintenance PIN, and your business card or a sticker with your number.

**Before you leave:** the loop for this screen is built and its ads are **approved**.

## 1. Pick the spot (with the owner)
- [ ] Where customers wait or stand in line, at eye level (center about 1.5 m / 5 ft up), in portrait.
- [ ] Within the cable's reach of an outlet that is **always on**: not on a light switch, and not on a power strip that's turned off at closing.
- [ ] No direct sun or strong glare on the screen. Away from sinks, heat, and doorways people brush past.
- [ ] Good WiFi there: check the bars on your phone in that exact spot.
- [ ] Doesn't block exits, signs or the owner's displays. The owner says yes to the spot.

## 2. Mount and power
- [ ] Mount firmly (locking enclosure, wall mount or strong adhesive frame), **portrait**, charging port reachable.
- [ ] Cover or hide the power and volume buttons if the mount allows.
- [ ] Route the cable along edges and clip it down, with no loops anyone can catch. Plug the charger in and confirm the ⚡ charging icon on the dashboard.

## 3. WiFi
- [ ] Ask for the **guest WiFi** name and password. It must not need a sign-in page (hotel- or café-style login).
- [ ] On the tablet: tap the corners clockwise from top-left, enter the PIN, then **Exit kiosk for 15 min**. Go to **WiFi**, connect, then press **Home** and choose **Back to kiosk mode now**.
- [ ] Write the network name in the screen's address notes on the dashboard.

## 4. Pair and check (on your phone)
- [ ] Not paired yet? **Screens → Pair new screen**: enter the code on the tablet, a screen name, the venue, its address and the loop.
- [ ] Within a minute the dashboard shows the screen **online**, **in sync** and **charging**, on the map at the right place.
- [ ] Watch one full loop on the tablet: every ad shows, upright, with no black screens.
- [ ] Brightness up, sound as the owner prefers (maintenance mode → Settings). Then press Home, swipe the edges, and press power once: it must stay on the ads.

## 5. Tell the owner
- "It runs by itself: it turns on, updates and restarts on its own. You never need to touch it."
- "Please keep it **plugged in** and don't move or cover it. If the power goes out, it comes back on its own."
- "If it's ever black, check that it's plugged in and the WiFi is working. If you change the WiFi password, call me."
- "We monitor it remotely and get an alert if it goes offline, so we may call or stop by."
- "Customers who ask about advertising on it: here's my card." Leave your card or sticker next to the screen.

## 6. Before you go
- [ ] Take a photo of the installed screen. Save the owner's name and phone and the install date (dashboard address notes).
- [ ] Check the dashboard once more from the parking lot: still online and playing.
